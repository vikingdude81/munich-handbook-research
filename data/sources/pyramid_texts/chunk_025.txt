Such meanings of nx(j) as "grow up, mature, evolve" can be taken as secondary. The same
phenomenon of secondary meaning occurs in the verb sAj "become sated", of which the secondary
meaning is "become wise, experienced" (cf. the determinative of sAA "experienced" (§854b N) and
sAAt "experienced" (§1701b N) which is {man with hand to mouth} – the same has sAj "become sated"
(§551c,e MN)). The meaning of the verbs having the root nx with regard to a child is clear from
following passages:

CT VI, 247i-k: Sd n.T N tp wart.T jmj n.f mnD.T pw rdj.n.T n.f s.nxn.T N jm.f
"Take N onto your thigh, give him this your breast you used to give him, that you may rear N with it"

Sethy I, Abydos III, pl. 48: jnk jt(j).k rdj.n(.j) [tw] m jb.j Dr nx.k Hr mnd(.j)
"I am your father, I have put [you] in my heart since you were growing up (lit. lasting) from (lit. on)
my breast".
