mj.k/mj.T/mj.Tn "See, Look, Behold". But the author of the current work thinks that "it was originally
followed by dependent pronouns…rather than the suffix forms" should be altered to "there are cases in
Old Egyptian when it is followed by dependent pronouns, next to cases that have the suffixal forms",
as these PT passages show. And this means that we actually have ancient 2-lit. verb mj "see" (for the
