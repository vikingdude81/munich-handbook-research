Pandemonium Matrix is a comprehensive system that encapsulates Lemurian demonism and time sorcery, incorporating two main elements: the Numogram (time-map) and the Matrix (a catalogue detailing the names, numbers, and characteristics of demons). The system is structured according to the inherent properties of decimal numeracy and incorporates fundamental arithmetic operations.

== Overview ==
The '''Numogram''', or Decimal Labyrinth, is constructed from ten zones numbered 0-9 and their links. These zones are grouped into five pairs, or syzygies, by a twinning process known as zygonovism. Each syzygy's arithmetic difference defines a current, or link to a tractor zone. These currents are the numogram's main flows.

The '''Matrix''' part of the system lists the names, numbers, and attributes of the demons, creating a vast demonological codex. Each demon, or entity, within the Matrix is uniquely identified by a specific zone-net couple, assigned a particular tonality or pitch, and defined by its role within the system.

== System Components ==
The entire Pandemonium Matrix system is comprised of several interconnected and interdependent components:

=== 1. Time-Systems ===
Three primary time-systems are formed by the arrangement of currents in the Numogram:

* '''Time-Circuit''': A cycle created by the mutual composition of the currents of the central syzygies, rotating in anticlockwise steps.
* '''Warp and Plex''': Currents in both upper and lower syzygies fold back into themselves, creating individual loops. They are seen as being of intrinsically cryptic nature, representing Outer-time.

=== 2. Gates and Channels ===
Each zone number in the numogram, when digitally cumulated, defines a gate's value. These gates and their corresponding channels form the secondary flows or secret interconnections in the numogram, creating routes for sorcerous traffic.

=== 3. Demons and Distances ===
The demons are singular entities characterized by irreducible distances between any pair of zones. Each demon is simultaneously a gap, link, hole, and entity with specific traits, features, aspects, and potentials.

== Demonic Groupings ==
Demons are grouped according to several properties:

* '''Chronodemons''': These represent varieties of distance within the time-circuit.
* '''Amphidemons''': They represent ruptures in the time-circuit, signifying openings to the Outside.
* '''Xenodemons''': These are considered the inhabitants of the outer abysses.

In addition to these categories, demons are also grouped by phase, each opened by a door attributed with a domain, planetary affinity, and spinal level.

== The Rites ==
The rites represent the various paths that the demons trace through the hyper-time of the maze. Each demon's rite is determined by how its net-span can be integrated or traced across the numogram's flows. Each rite corresponds to a particular omen and power.

== Necronomicon ==
In Western hyperfictional lore, the Matrix of all demons (Pandemonium) is called Necronomicon, or The Book of Dead Names. This text lists all entities excluded from the white-magic book of life, and it's said to be a copy of an original document that was retroactively sent from the future into the distant past, thus mixing elements of science fiction with ancient legend.

== General Information ==

* Author: Peter Vysparov
* Translator: Chaim Horowitz
* First Published: August 12, 2004

== Overview ==
Following Chaim Horowitz’s translation of the ancient text, the Book of Paths, Peter Vysparov embarked on an extensive effort to decipher the cryptic script. His journey was guided by Horowitz's hypothesis, suggesting a potential correlation between the text and the rites of Lemurian demonism outlined in his Pandemonium Matrix. Despite a few minor discrepancies, the hypothesis was found to be accurate. The concordance was reportedly completed in the early 1960s.

The Vysparov Library, custodians of the Book of Paths, made available a copy of the concordance for further studies and research.

Decimal numeracy (or base-ten positional numeral system) is the most widespread, historically and currently, system for denoting integer and real numbers. It is of especial importance for Ccru being a base for [[Barker Spiral]] and the [[Numogram]] (or Decimal Labyrinth).

Most historic numeral systems are decimal due to the human body plan. Hindu-Arabic positional decimal numeracy became a standard for much of the contemporary world. It is very conceptually compact in using the same set of ten digits in each position; this also means that digits are scale-indifferent. Also notably, positional numeracy explicitly notates [[Zero|zero]].

The distinction between the terms '''numeric''' and '''numerical''' is that the first denotes things consisting of numbers, and the second, things having to do, e.g. operating on, numbers. '''Numeral''' is a noun meaning the way of writing down a number with digits, characters, etc — and the ''number'' itself is a mathematical object.

The Book of Paths is a mysterious work tied to Lemurian demonism and time sorcery, which was first referenced within the Kaye Materials obtained by the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit (Ccru) in January 2000. Despite being referred to as the "definitive confirmation of Stillwell’s basic insight," the Ccru was initially unable to locate the text. The Book of Paths, while a rare and cryptic text, stands as a testament to the intriguing web of occult history and the continuing allure of the unknown. The Ccru's discovery and subsequent dissemination of the text represent a significant contribution to understanding the intricacies of Lemurian demonism and time sorcery.

== Discovery ==
The first breakthrough in accessing the Book of Paths came in 2003 during an investigation by the Ccru into references linking William Burroughs, Peter Vysparov, and Lemurian time travel. The research led the Ccru to correspond with the Vysparov estate, primarily focusing on the contents of the Vysparov Library, where William Kaye had previously worked.

The Vysparov Library, notorious for its collection of occult works, eventually yielded a photocopy of The Book of Paths. The document came with an attached page from a letter penned by Chaim Horowitz to Peter Vysparov, dated February 1949. It was identified as the 'B Manuscript' of The Book of Paths, being one of three type-copies of Horowitz’s first English translation from Tibetan.

== The Letter ==
In his letter to Vysparov, Horowitz indicates that translating the Old Book took over a year and required some difficult choices to preserve the work's systematic coherence. He hints at the troubled circumstances under which he obtained the copy from 'the place,' believed by William Kaye to be a concealed sub-basement of the Potala Palace in Lhasa.

Horowitz also notes Echidna Stillwell's interesting findings about the text's history, tracing it back to the Warring States period in China. Even at that time, the book was considered profoundly ancient. There were suggestions that it predated the I Ching and rumors that the text was destroyed in Chin Shi Huang Di’s great burning, considered one of the yaoshu or 'devilish books.'

Echidna Stillwell believed that the book served as an oracle for the Shu Kingdom 5,000 years ago and mentioned persistent rumors about 84 bronze tablets removed from the Shu excavation site by 'looters' or 'senior officials.'

Despite the translation's crude production, Horowitz expresses hope that the text would prove invaluable to Vysparov's work on the 'Pandemonium System,' though as of May 2004, the Ccru had not determined any such profound relevance.

== Notes on Key Figures ==

==== '''Chaim Horowitz''' ====
A researcher who worked in the Mu Archives, located possibly in a concealed sub-basement of the Potala Palace in Lhasa, Tibet. He was the primary translator of The Book of Paths.

==== '''Echidna Stillwell''' ====
An esteemed Lemurologist who met with Horowitz in Rangoon in 1949 and who found traces of The Book of Paths' history within Chinese sources dating back to the Warring States period.

==== '''Madame Centauri''' ====
Referred to as "Mme C." in the letter, likely Zelda Maria de Monterre, a fortune teller in New York. Horowitz hoped that she might shed light on the oracular dimension of The Book of Paths.

==== '''Peter Vysparov''' ====
A scholar with whom Horowitz corresponded, Vysparov is linked to William Burroughs and Lemurian time travel, a topic of research for the Ccru.
