The '''Architectonic Order of the Eschaton (AOE)''' is an extremely hermetic magical society, cloaked in layers of secrecy, even from its own initiates. Established in the abysms of antiquity over a game of cards, the AOE claims to antedate every existing tradition of human wisdom, while preserving within its ranks the seed of ultimate terrestrial Gnosis.

== Philosophy and Mission ==
The AOE's mission centers on the establishment and fortification of the institutions of time, using the Oecumenic Calendar as a sign and register of its Great Work. The society is bound by divine covenant to marshal the forces of light against Lemurian polyculture, combating all its various forms of time-sorcery, demon-traffic, and endless untime.

A critical period in AOE activity was observed as AD 2000 approached, a time marked by increased theoconservatism, hardening MS-monopolism, financial sabotage of Pacific economies, the consolidation of the EU-Metastate, and the unleashing of Gregorian Restoration. The society anticipates, and possibly programs, climactic confrontations with its enemies at specific historical junctions.

== Tactics and Strategies ==
A multifaceted approach is employed by the AOE to remain hidden and obscure their activities. Techniques range from inducing generalized psychotic paranoia through intense semi-random persecutions, to conducting orchestrated micro-campaigns. Conspiracy theory fatigue is also achieved at the macro-social level with selective diversionary assassinations designed for complex interpretations.

Additionally, the AOE camouflages itself behind layers of pseudo-magic, typified by grand celestial wizards, incantations, and an authoritarian slant on mysticism. This deception is further enhanced by organizing periodic theatrical assassinations of major religious figures.

== Magic and Power ==
At its core, the AOE believes in the power of serious magic, conceptualized as boxes within boxes, endless embeddings, and concentric closings of circles. It posits the universe as a fabrication, an ultimate sealed-system and organized unity, obedient to pre-established laws.

AOE magic involves producing effective spells or command-codes through the metatronic naturalization of restricted reality structures. It understands that the past can be constructed to colonize the future, thereby installing authoritative Will in the decision of Being. This process evolves from created substance to programmable technicity.

== Influence and Legacy ==
The AOE is rooted in the White Atlantean tradition, where an initial lie is transformed into chip-architecture, lensing through Plato. The tradition inherits a metafictional relay-series, spreading over nine millennia of pre-packaged fake-time. This process allows the AOE to implant massive artificial memory following catastrophic erasures of literate culture.

In essence, the AOE embodies the Alpha-to-Omega, the Empire of the Magical-Sign, a white-brotherhood waging a timeless war against the forces of chaos and disorder. However, given the society's inherent secrecy and hermetic nature, all reported facts about its history, organization, doctrine, and practices should be treated with extraordinary skepticism.
