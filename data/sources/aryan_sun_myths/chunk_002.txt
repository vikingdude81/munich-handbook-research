They had a

The

The

"the

The Persians believe that Zoroaster, the founder of

While

If we

the Aryan sun-myths became the foundation of their

One of

The facts relating to the

It was said that he was born on

At the

" The

Like
other
sun-gods, he met with

over

was

63
finally conquered by his

At
the annual

His image

the temple, with black veils

The Mourning Song, whose plaintive tones

His tomb

(See NOTE 5.)
His

Egypt,

great
exemplar of self-sacrifice, in giving his life for others,
as the Manifester

Good,

Opener
of

The Egyptian Book of the Dead, the oldest Bible in

On

These

contemporary
with the building of the pyramids, which were

is said to have been born.
Osiris was represented

In one

" I

" The Word

The monogram of Osiris

His symbol
