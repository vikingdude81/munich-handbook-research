The insistence on secrecy highlights a dimension of the occult arts that
might otherwise be less clear: their value simply as a form of knowledge.
From the viewpoint of these writers, knowledge might bring power but it
also was power. Knowing mysterious things was in itself valuable, even if
the knowledge was never put to effect in action. In the extreme cases

brings hidden things to light, or at least to the dim visibility of the
shadows.
